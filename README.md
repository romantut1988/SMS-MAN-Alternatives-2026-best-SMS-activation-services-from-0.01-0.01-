### How much does SMS activation cost?

Paid SMS activation can start at around $0.01 for some services and countries. Rental numbers can start at around $0.50. Actual prices depend on the provider, country, target service, and rental period.

### Are free temporary phone numbers safe?

Free numbers are public and shared. Other users may be able to see incoming SMS messages, and the number may have been used previously. They are better suited to basic testing and low-risk verification than private accounts.

### Why might a verification SMS not arrive?

The number may already have a registration history with the service, the provider may have limited availability, the target service may block temporary numbers, or the SMS may be delayed.

If a code does not arrive, check the provider's status and consider another available number or provider.

### Can temporary numbers receive voice calls?

Some providers support voice verification and automated calls. This feature is not available for every provider, country, or service.

### Can developers use SMS activation APIs?

Yes. Some providers offer APIs for automated SMS verification workflows, app testing, onboarding, and QA. API pricing, limits, supported countries, and available services vary between providers.

### Is SMS activation legal?

The legality depends on the jurisdiction, provider rules, and how the number is used. Temporary numbers should not be used to bypass laws, identity requirements, platform restrictions, or other security controls.
